Jamii Hospital

Jamii Hospital is a healthcare institution in Karatina, Nyeri County, dedicated to providing quality, compassionate and affordable care to our community. We are located on Kuguru Road in the Mahiga / Sigoni area, next to PanAfric Girls High School.

Our qualified team of doctors, clinicians and nurses is devoted to delivering high-quality care. Whether through inpatient services, outpatient care, or specialized consultations, we provide timely and personalized medical attention to meet each patient's unique needs.

Our vision is to be the hospital of choice in the provision of quality health care for all.
