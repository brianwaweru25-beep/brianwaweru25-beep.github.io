About Us

Health care you can trust, close to home.

Who We Are

Jamii Hospital is a healthcare institution in Karatina, Nyeri County, located on Kuguru Road in the Mahiga / Sigoni area, next to PanAfric Girls High School. We are dedicated to providing quality, compassionate and affordable healthcare to our community.

Our team of doctors, clinicians and nurses delivers inpatient, outpatient and emergency care, supported by laboratory, pharmacy, dental and paediatric services.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be the hospital of choice in the provision of quality health care for all.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, compassionate and professional healthcare to every patient we serve.
